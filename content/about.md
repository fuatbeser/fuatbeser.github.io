Fuat is a professional with extensive experience in technology and digital transformation. He has contributed to the development of initiatives that integrate technology with practical business impact.

## Professional Contributions

Fuat co-founded the Swiss-based AI Business School, where he has helped support corporations around the globe in their digital transformation efforts. The school employs an automated, scalable SaaS (Software as a Service) platform that emphasizes efficiency and the practical application of innovation. He is leading product teams at AI Business School, with a focus on the AI Adoption product. AI Adoption is a SaaS product designed to drive mass AI adoption for companies, enabling them to leverage artificial intelligence to enhance their operations, improve decision-making, and gain a competitive edge in their respective industries.

In addition to his work with the AI Business School, Fuat is a co-founder of the Swiss-based Global AI Hub. This initiative is designed to provide a structured environment where professionals in artificial intelligence and technology can further develop their skills and advance their careers. The Global AI Hub offers a range of resources, including training programs, networking opportunities, and access to cutting-edge research, to help individuals stay at the forefront of AI advancements.

## Community Involvement and Recognition

Beyond his professional roles, Fuat has been active in various volunteer and community-based initiatives. He founded the **Deep Learning Turkey** community, which brings together individuals interested in artificial intelligence (AI). This community serves as a platform for knowledge sharing, collaboration, and the promotion of AI research and development within Turkey. His expertise in machine learning also earned him recognition as a **Google Developer Expert**, a title awarded to individuals who demonstrate exceptional knowledge and contributions to the developer community.

Fuat has also contributed to projects aimed at enhancing the accessibility of educational content on the **Coursera** platform, focusing on the Turkish language. His work as an **AI Ambassador** at **deeplearning.ai** has enabled him to share knowledge and support others interested in artificial intelligence, fostering a global community of AI enthusiasts and professionals.
